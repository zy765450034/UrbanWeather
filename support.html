Forecast is missing or stale预报缺失或未更新

  1. Confirm your Mac has an internet connection.确认 Mac 已连接互联网。
  2. Choose the city again or search for a nearby place.重新选择该城市，或搜索附近地点。
  3. Quit and reopen UrbanWeather if a cached forecast remains visible.如果仍显示缓存预报，请退出并重新打开都市天气。

Open-Meteo coverage and update timing vary by location. Temporary provider interruptions can prevent a refresh even when your connection works.Open-Meteo 的覆盖范围与更新时间因地点而异。即使网络正常，服务商临时中断也可能导致刷新失败。

Current location does not work当前位置无法使用

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and confirm access for UrbanWeather. If you prefer not to grant location access, search for a city or postal code instead.打开“系统设置”→“隐私与安全性”→“定位服务”，确认已允许都市天气使用定位。若不希望授权定位，也可以搜索城市或邮政编码。

Radar is empty or unavailable雷达为空或不可用

Radar needs both Apple Maps and RainViewer. Check your connection, then close and reopen radar. RainViewer coverage, frame freshness and service availability vary by place and time. The timeline contains recent historical precipitation frames, not a future nowcast.雷达同时依赖 Apple 地图与 RainViewer。请检查网络后关闭并重新打开雷达。RainViewer 的覆盖范围、画面时效与服务可用性会因地点和时间而异。时间轴显示近期历史降水画面，并非未来临近预报。

Language, units or AQI scale语言、单位或 AQI 标准

Open UrbanWeather Settings to choose among twelve interface languages, set regional or explicit measurement units, and select the US or European air-quality scale. Restart the app if a menu-bar label has not refreshed.打开都市天气设置，可在 12 种界面语言中选择，设置地区默认或指定计量单位，并选择美国或欧洲空气质量标准。如果菜单栏文字未更新，请重新启动应用。

Include your macOS version, UrbanWeather version, selected city, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not include precise coordinates or other sensitive information because requests are public.请提供 macOS 版本、都市天气版本、所选城市与复现步骤。支持请求为公开内容，请勿包含精确坐标或其他敏感信息。
