Your forecast, without a profile.获取预报，无需建立档案。

UrbanWeather does not require an account, run advertising, use analytics, or create an analytics or advertising identifier. This policy explains the requests needed to provide weather and radar.都市天气无需账户，不投放广告、不使用分析工具，也不会创建分析或广告标识符。本政策说明为提供天气与雷达功能所需的网络请求。

What UrbanWeather handles都市天气处理哪些信息

The app handles a city search, coordinates you choose or permit, weather responses, saved cities, preferences and temporary cache data. It uses these only to deliver the features you request.应用会处理城市搜索文字、您选择或授权的位置坐标、天气响应、收藏城市、偏好设置与临时缓存数据，仅用于提供您所请求的功能。

No developer account or tracking backend无开发者账户或跟踪后端

UrbanWeather has no sign-in system and sends no data to a developer-owned analytics, advertising or account server.都市天气没有登录系统，也不会向开发者自有的分析、广告或账户服务器发送数据。

Requests to service providers向服务商发出的请求

Open-Meteo

When you search for a place, search text is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. When you request weather or air quality, the selected city's coordinates—or coordinates obtained after you grant macOS location permission—are sent to Open-Meteo. Open-Meteo returns forecast, weather and air-quality data. Open-Meteo states that free-API server logs may contain IP addresses and sensitive request information such as coordinates, and that these logs are deleted after 90 days and are not shared with third parties.搜索地点时，搜索文字会发送至 Open-Meteo 地理编码服务。请求天气或空气质量时，所选城市的坐标，或您授权 macOS 定位后获得的坐标，会发送至 Open-Meteo。Open-Meteo 随后返回预报、天气与空气质量数据。Open-Meteo 说明免费接口的服务器日志可能包含 IP 地址及坐标等敏感请求信息，日志会在 90 天后删除且不会与第三方共享。

RainViewer

When you open historical radar, the app requests RainViewer metadata and precipitation map tiles. Requests necessarily include network information such as your IP address. UrbanWeather displays past frames from roughly the previous two hours and does not use RainViewer's future nowcast frames.打开历史雷达时，应用会请求 RainViewer 元数据与降水地图图块。请求必然包含 IP 地址等网络信息。都市天气显示大约过去两小时的历史画面，不使用 RainViewer 的未来临近预报画面。

Apple Maps

Opening radar also loads the underlying map through Apple Maps. Apple receives the map requests and associated network information needed to return map content.打开雷达时还会通过 Apple 地图加载底图。Apple 会接收返回地图内容所需的地图请求及相关网络信息。

These independent providers process requests under their own terms and privacy practices. UrbanWeather cannot control provider records. RainViewer's exact retention practices must be confirmed for the release.这些独立服务商依据各自条款与隐私惯例处理请求，都市天气无法控制服务商保存的记录。正式发布时仍需确认 RainViewer 的具体数据保留方式。

Data stored on your Mac保存在 Mac 上的数据

Saved cities, their order, language, units, AQI preference and other settings remain in the app's local sandbox. Forecast responses are cached locally to improve resilience and loading speed. This data is not synchronized to an UrbanWeather account or developer server.收藏城市及其顺序、语言、单位、AQI 偏好与其他设置保存在应用本地沙盒中。天气响应也会在本地缓存，以提升稳定性与加载速度。这些数据不会同步至都市天气账户或开发者服务器。

Your control and deletion您的控制权与删除方式

  • macOS controls location permission. You can revoke it in System Settings; city search remains available.定位权限由 macOS 管理。您可以在系统设置中撤销权限，城市搜索仍可使用。
  • Removing a saved city removes it from the app's saved list.删除收藏城市会将其从应用列表中移除。
  • Deleting the app and its application data removes locally stored settings and cache. Provider request records, if any, are governed by each provider.删除应用及其应用数据会移除本地设置与缓存。服务商可能留存的请求记录由各服务商管理。

Questions and changes问题与政策变更

For privacy questions, open a request through the UrbanWeather support page. This policy will be updated if providers or data handling change.如有隐私问题，请通过都市天气支持页提交请求。如服务商或数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将随之更新。
